# UiPath review ledger

Fix the comparison point: approved plan, review feedback, branch, commit, or merge base. Invoke `$code-review` for its Standards and Spec process, then trace each requirement through active XAML or coded workflows, invokes, public arguments, `Config.xlsx`, entry-point metadata, test variations, and externally visible outputs.

Classify every requirement as `implemented`, `partial`, `missing`, `superseded`, or `residual risk`. An architectural substitution remains open until the reviewer accepts it. Closure requires every row to have source evidence and the appropriate L1, L2, L3, or UAT state.
